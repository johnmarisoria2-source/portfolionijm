The Art & Soul of PERFOOM

Our Story

Born from a passion for scent as a form of art

PERFOOM was founded on a single belief: that fragrance is one of the most intimate forms of self-expression. What you wear on your skin tells a story — of where you've been, who you are, and who you aspire to become.

We began as a small atelier, blending rare ingredients sourced from the world's most extraordinary places — Bulgarian rose fields, Indian sandalwood forests, the resin-soaked markets of Oman. Today, every bottle we create carries that same devotion to craft and origin.

The Person Behind the Scent

John Mari S. Soria
Founder & CEO

A dream that started with a single bottle.

My name is John Mari S. Soria, and I am the founder and CEO of PERFOOM. What started as a personal fascination with fragrance grew into something I never expected — a brand built on the belief that scent is one of the most powerful ways to express who you are.

PERFOOM was established in 2024, born out of a simple frustration: luxury fragrance felt out of reach for most people. I wanted to change that — to create a space where quality, elegance, and affordability could exist together without compromise.

From curating our first collection out of a small room to building an online store that reaches customers nationwide, every step of this journey has been driven by passion and purpose. I believe every person deserves a signature scent — one that tells their story without saying a word.

John Mari S. Soria
